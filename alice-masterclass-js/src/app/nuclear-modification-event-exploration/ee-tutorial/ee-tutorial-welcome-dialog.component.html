Event Exploration

Welcome, young apprentice of physics!

In heavy ion collisions a hot, dense medium can form. High momentum particles lose energy in that medium (jet quenching). The nuclear modification factor RAA compares particle production in PbPb to proton proton collisions.

This exercise builds intuition on single events: you will look at real collisions and learn to keep only the charged primary tracks, the same objects real ALICE analyses count for RAA.
