Event Characteristics histograms

These six plots fill as you analyse events. Here is what each one shows:

Multiplicity
Number of accepted primary tracks per event. The Entries / Mean box summarises the histogram.
Multiplicity, pT > 1 GeV/c
Same multiplicity idea, but only tracks with transverse momentum above 1 GeV/c, more sensitive to jet quenching.
Multiplicity of secondaries
How many non primary tracks were in the event. These must not enter the RAA yield; this plot reminds you what you are rejecting.
pT distribution
Transverse momentum spectrum of the tracks you accepted.
Charge distribution
Sign of the charge (±1) for accepted tracks.
φ distribution
Azimuthal angle φ of accepted tracks around the beam axis.
