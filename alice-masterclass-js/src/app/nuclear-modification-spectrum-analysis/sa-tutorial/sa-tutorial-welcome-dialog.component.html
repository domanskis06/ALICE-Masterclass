Spectrum Analysis short tutorial

In the first exercise you looked at single collisions. Now you analyse the whole data sample: about 40 000 Pb–Pb events, sorted into ten centrality classes, plus a proton–proton reference measured by ALICE.

You build the analysis yourself out of blocks. Each block is one step a real physicist performs: take the tracks of a centrality class, fill a pT histogram, and then normalise it — per GeV/c of bin width, per event, and per number of collisions — before dividing by the pp reference to get RAA.

Nothing is prepared for you: an incomplete chain gives a wrong answer, and the module tells you what is missing. You can skip this tour anytime with ×, and the ? buttons above the workspace and the plots open short guides you can read whenever you are stuck.
